Start here

If you are not sure where to look:

  1. Not sure what to fight next? Open the Boss Order and tick off what you have already killed. Every other page then knows where you are: Loadouts opens on your checkpoint, and the recipe pages can hide what you cannot make yet.
  2. About to fight a boss? Go to Loadouts, click the boss on the timeline, then your class. You get one panel of what to equip — and each pick says why it was chosen. Arrow keys walk the run; the full list is a click below.
  3. Wondering what an item combines into? The Tinkerer's Workshop page shows every accessory combination, and flags the 9 recipes the mods change from vanilla.
  4. Looking for a specific recipe? All Recipes searches every item in the pack at once, and can filter to what is craftable before Hardmode.

What's in the pack

Six content mods, plus the vanilla Tinkerer's Workshop where their accessories meet.

Thorium Mod

2,151 recipes

11 bosses, ~2,600 items, and the Bard, Healer and Thrower classes.

Fargo's Souls / Mutant

1,395 recipes

Eternity Mode rewrites every vanilla boss. Boss summons and re-fights.

Spirit Reforged

662 recipes

Biomes, events and atmosphere, built for multiplayer.

Spirit Classic

665 recipes

~12 bosses. Added last, after a clean join test.

The Stars Above

224 recipes

9 bosses, Hardmode to post-Moon Lord. Native Thorium damage support.

Calamity Fables

201 recipes

A standalone Calamity reimagining. 3 pre-Hardmode bosses.

Before and after Hardmode

Every recipe is checked against the wikis to see whether you can actually make it yet. 3,149 are craftable before Hardmode; 2,306 need Hardmode content.

How it was built

Recipes are parsed from each wiki's own generated recipe tables through the MediaWiki API — 161 station pages across seven wikis. Loadouts come from each wiki's Guide:Class setups. Nothing is written from memory.

Offline

Every page is self-contained with its sprites embedded, so the wiki works with the Wi-Fi off — handy on a second monitor mid-session.
